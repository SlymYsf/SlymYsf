## About Me

- I am a **Information Systems Engineering student** and an **aspiring full-stack developer**.
- I am **20 years old** and based in **İstanbul, Türkiye**.
- I am studying at **Kocaeli University**, Information Systems Engineering.
- I recently completed a **mandatory internship at Kodpit Teknoloji A.Ş.**.
- My daily stack is **PHP, Laravel, MySQL, vanilla JS/CSS, Blade, and PHPUnit**.
- During this summer's internship, I developed the **Devlet Havayolları** project — I built it to learn the topics I've been studying recently (Laravel migrations, Eloquent relationships, the service class pattern, pricing/yield management, PNR generation, database transactions, pessimistic locking, caching, query optimization, and MVC architecture) by applying them to a real-world scenario.
